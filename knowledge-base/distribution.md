# BookLeaf Worldwide Distribution Policy

## 1. Distribution Channels
- **Domestic (India)**: BookLeaf Online Store, Amazon India, Flipkart.
- **Global**: Amazon Kindle Worldwide, IngramSpark, Kobo, Google Play Books.

## 2. Listing Timelines & Sync
- Once published, domestic listings go live on Amazon and Flipkart within **7 to 10 business days**.
- Global distribution via IngramSpark updates across 39,000+ bookstores worldwide within **14 to 21 business days**.
- Inventory status on third-party platforms is synchronized weekly.
