# BookLeaf ISBN Assignment & Registration

## 1. Overview
- Every book published under BookLeaf Publishing receives a free 13-digit **ISBN (International Standard Book Number)** registered with the Raja Rammohun Roy National Agency for ISBN (Government of India).

## 2. Timeline & Process
- ISBN registration is initiated immediately after the manuscript formatting and cover design receive author approval.
- ISBN assignment typically takes **3 to 7 business days**.
- Draft books (e.g. status "Draft" or "In Review") display ISBN as "Pending" until official assignment is complete.

## 3. Barcode & Copyright Page
- The assigned ISBN and barcode are automatically rendered on the back cover and copyright page of the published paperback and hardcover editions.
- Authors do not need to purchase a separate ISBN.
