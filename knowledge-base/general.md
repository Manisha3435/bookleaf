# BookLeaf General Author Support

## 1. Portal Support & Contact Hours
- BookLeaf Support desk operates Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM IST.
- Average response time for ticket queries is **24 hours**. Urgent and Critical priority tickets are addressed within **4 to 6 hours**.

## 2. Managing Author Account & Security
- Authors can update their personal details, phone number, and bank accounts directly via the Author Portal profile settings.
- For security reasons, ticket inquiries must be submitted through the authenticated Author Support Portal.
