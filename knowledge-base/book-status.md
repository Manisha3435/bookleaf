# Book Status Workflow & Lifecycle

## 1. Status Definitions
- **Draft**: Manuscript uploaded by author, undergoing initial setup or formatting review. MRP, publication date, and sales metrics are null.
- **In Review**: Manuscript and cover files under review by BookLeaf editorial and publishing operations team.
- **Published**: Book has completed formatting, ISBN assignment, and is live across active sales channels.
- **Out of Print**: Book has been decommissioned or withdrawn from sale at author request.

## 2. In-Progress Books (Draft & In Review)
- Books in "Draft" or "In Review" status will naturally show `MRP = null`, `copiesSold = null`, and `publicationDate = null`. This is normal expected behavior and not a system error.
