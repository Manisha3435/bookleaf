# BookLeaf Royalty Policy & Payout Guidelines

## 1. Royalty Calculation
- Authors earn **70% of Net Profits** on print sales and **80% on eBooks** published through BookLeaf Publishing.
- Royalty calculations are finalized at the end of each calendar month.

## 2. Payout Schedule & Minimum Threshold
- **Payout Date**: Royalties are processed and disbursed on the **15th of every month** for sales recorded in previous billing cycles.
- **Minimum Threshold**: The minimum payout threshold is **₹1,000**.
- **Rollover Rule**: If an author's pending royalty balance is below **₹1,000** (for example, ₹850), the amount is NOT lost or forfeited. It automatically rolls over to the next billing month until the accumulated pending royalty reaches or exceeds ₹1,000.

## 3. Payout Status & Overdue Inquiries
- **Zero Pending (₹0)**: Means all earned royalties to date have been fully calculated and transferred to the author's registered bank account.
- **Zero Paid & Null Date**: For newly published books or books with sales under processing, payouts will be triggered on the next 15th payout date once sales reports are reconciled.
- **Overdue or Delayed Royalties**: If pending royalties exceed ₹1,000 and have passed the 15th payout date without credit, our finance team requires verification. Inquiries regarding delayed royalties are escalated to the Finance Desk with a standard **48-hour resolution timeline**.

## 4. Bank Account & Tax Compliance
- Payouts are made directly via NEFT/IMPS to the bank account updated in the Author Profile.
- TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) is applied as per Indian Income Tax regulations (10% under Section 194J/194IB where applicable).
