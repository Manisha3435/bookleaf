# BookLeaf Printing & Proofing Guidelines

## 1. Print-on-Demand (POD) Model
- BookLeaf operates on a high-quality Print-on-Demand (POD) model. Books are printed when ordered, eliminating inventory wastage and keeping books perpetually in stock.

## 2. Print Quality Specifications
- **Interior**: 70-80 GSM Natural Shade or White Paper.
- **Cover**: 300 GSM Art Card with Matte or Gloss Lamination.
- **Binding**: Perfect Binding for Paperbacks; Case Binding for Hardcovers.

## 3. Author Proof Copies & Turnaround
- Authors receive 1 complimentary physical author copy upon initial publication.
- Print turnaround time is **3 to 5 business days** from order placement, followed by **2 to 4 days shipping** across India.
